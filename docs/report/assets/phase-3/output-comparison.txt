VI SAO KHONG CHUAN HOA BANG str.splitlines()
==============================================================================

splitlines() cat dong tren 11 ky tu chu khong phai 1. Neu dung no, mot bai
in ra ky tu dieu khien se duoc coi nhu da xuong dong va an diem oan:

  vertical tab       splitlines() coi la bang: True    he thong: False
  form feed          splitlines() coi la bang: True    he thong: False
  NEL (U+0085)       splitlines() coi la bang: True    he thong: False
  LINE SEPARATOR     splitlines() coi la bang: True    he thong: False

=> chuan hoa chi tach tren LF, sau khi quy CRLF va CR ve LF.
